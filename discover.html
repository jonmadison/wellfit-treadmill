FTMS Treadmill Discovery

Connects, enumerates the Fitness Machine Service, and decodes what your treadmill actually supports. Read-only except for the explicitly labelled probes.

Safety: stay off the belt while running discovery. Nothing here commands speed or incline, but Request Control can put the treadmill into a remote-controlled state.
Advanced: send a raw control-point opcode

Only use this with the treadmill clear and someone watching it. Opcodes like 07 (Start) will physically move the belt.
